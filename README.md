# About Me
I am an interdisciplinary data scientist and social impact leader dedicated to using advanced computational methods - from Data Science to Machine Learning - to address complex social problems. 

My career has come full circle: I began as a coder and a consultant, before taking-up Director level roles leading research and social innovation at organisations such as Pearson and the Queen Rania Foundation.

I then became obsessed with the potential of Data Science, Machine Learning and AI to make sense of the world.

Today, I bridge the gap between technical execution and high-level strategy. I like to think that I act as a bridge between different disciplines and outlooks on the world. I am currently expanding my skills through a Masters level qualification in Data Science, Machine Learning and AI at the University of Cambridge.
